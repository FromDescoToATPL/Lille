Bars and Nightlife in Lille

Bars & nightlife

Three neighbourhoods, three very different moods. Pick depending on what kind of evening you're after.

Rue Solférino — the student strip

Solférino / Masséna district

Lille's liveliest street after dark, packed with bars running long happy hours and cheap pints. It's loud, festive, and clearly aimed at a student crowd. Le Solférino (156 rue Solférino) is a welcoming bar-tabac with a small terrace and pub games; a little further along, Seven Bar (124 rue Solférino) is another neighbourhood regular.

Vieux-Lille — for a quieter drink

Historic centre

A noticeably calmer mood, with wine bars like Au Gré du Vin or Nectar Dégustation, where the point is discovering a good bottle rather than working through a round of shots. The stone-and-exposed-beam setting suits an evening well.

Wazemmes — the most eclectic district

Around place Sébastopol and the market

A mix of craft beer bars, cocktail spots and more alternative addresses, in a lively, multicultural neighbourhood. The mood shifts a lot from one street to the next, which makes it a good area for bar-hopping without a fixed plan.
