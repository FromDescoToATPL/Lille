What's on in Lille

The city's big fixtures, in the order they arrive.

Lille runs on seasons. Winter belongs to the Christmas Village and the Ferris wheel; on the first weekend of September the whole city turns into a giant flea market. In between, a handful of quieter events worth catching if your timing is right.

Coming up

The Christmas market and the Ferris wheel

November – December 2026 · Place Rihour and Grand'Place

Ninety wooden chalets on Place Rihour, a fifty-metre wheel on the Grand'Place, and six weeks of a fully lit city centre. Lille's biggest annual event by visitor numbers.

The rest of the year

The funfair, la Foire aux manèges

Twice a year: spring and late summer · Champ de Mars

The largest funfair in northern France takes over the Champ de Mars esplanade at the foot of the Citadelle. Free entry, paid rides, and an atmosphere that owes nothing to tourism: people here have been going since childhood.

Lille Street Food Festival

Every year in early October · EuraTechnologies

Four days of street food in a red-brick former spinning mill, with around fifty stalls and chefs from across the metropolis. Free entry, cashless payment, and enough variety to try a dozen things in one evening.

Bière à Lille beer festival (the BAL)

Every year in early November · Gare Saint-Sauveur and across the metropolis

A week of tastings in bars across the metropolis, mostly free to attend, followed by a closing weekend that brings nearly seventy breweries together under the glass roof of the Gare Saint-Sauveur.

La Braderie de Lille

Next edition: first weekend of September 2027

Thirty-four hours of non-stop browsing, two million visitors, and mountains of mussels and fries outside every restaurant. The 2026 edition has passed, but the guide still helps you prepare for the next one: how to plan, where to stay and what to skip.
