Itineraries

Three ways to plan a trip without building a schedule from scratch. Every place mentioned is covered in more detail elsewhere in the guide.

Lille in 24 hours

Morning
Vieux-Lille and the Grand'Place as shops open, then climb the Belfry
Lunch
An estaminet (Au Vieux de la Vieille or La Ch'tite Brigitte)
Afternoon
The Palais des Beaux-Arts, or the Citadelle park if you'd rather be outdoors
Evening
Dinner, then a drink on rue Solférino (lively) or a Vieux-Lille wine bar (quieter)

A weekend for foodies

Saturday morning
Wazemmes market, the liveliest in the city, to get a feel for local life
Saturday lunch
Carbonnade or welsh at a Vieux-Lille estaminet
Saturday afternoon
A waffle break at Meert, then a wander down rue de Gand
Saturday evening
Something more contemporary, like Le Barbier qui fume
Sunday
Day trip to Brussels (35 min by train) for Belgian beer and chocolate, just to compare

What to do in Lille when it rains

Morning
The Palais des Beaux-Arts, easily enough to fill 1.5 hours indoors
Lunch
A long lunch at an estaminet — exactly the kind of place made for lingering
Afternoon
Notre-Dame-de-la-Treille cathedral, then the covered halls of Wazemmes market
Evening
A cosy Vieux-Lille wine bar rather than an outdoor terrace
