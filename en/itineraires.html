Ready-made itineraries

Three ways to plan a stay without starting from scratch. Every place mentioned is covered elsewhere in the guide.

Lille in 24 hours

Morning
Vieux-Lille and the Grand'Place as the shops open, then up the Belfry
Lunch
An estaminet, Au Vieux de la Vieille or La Ch'tite Brigitte
Afternoon
The Palais des Beaux-Arts, or the Citadelle and its park if you'd rather stay outside
Evening
Dinner, then a drink on rue Solférino if you want noise, or in a Vieux-Lille wine bar if you want quiet

A weekend for food lovers

Saturday morning
Wazemmes market, the liveliest in the city
Saturday lunch
Carbonnade or welsh in a Vieux-Lille estaminet
Saturday afternoon
A waffle at Meert, then a wander down rue de Gand
Saturday evening
Somewhere more modern, like Le Barbier qui fume
Sunday
Brussels, 35 minutes by train, to compare the beer and the chocolate

What to do in Lille when it rains

Morning
The Palais des Beaux-Arts, there's enough for an hour and a half indoors
Lunch
A long lunch in an estaminet. It's exactly the kind of place you linger in
Afternoon
Notre-Dame-de-la-Treille cathedral, then the covered halls of Wazemmes market
Evening
A Vieux-Lille wine bar rather than a terrace
