Who writes this guide?

I've lived in Lille since I was a child. I've watched the city change, reinvent itself, and I've ended up knowing most of its corners.

I live in Vieux-Lille. Welsh and carbonnade are second nature by now: at Estaminet La Vieille France or Au Vieux de la Vieille. But I'm just as keen on Asian food, Thai or Korean — nothing stops me. That's Lille too: it isn't all northern French cooking.

And then there are the bars. They're probably what I know best about this city — my picks are on the Bars page.

Why this guide

To let anyone, French or not, find the parts of the city you don't stumble on by accident. I wanted to write what I'd tell a friend arriving for three days.

The site is in French and English. Lille is an hour from Brussels, under two from London, and plenty of people passing through don't speak French — so I may as well be useful to them.

The places mentioned here are simply the ones I go to. None of them paid to be listed, and if a link ever earns me anything, it will be clearly marked.
